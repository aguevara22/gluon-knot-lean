# Literature sources — index of every assumed interface (frame SM12; registry unchanged since the exit source pass on SM6)

This index maps each admitted input of the Supplemental Material to the exact source version the campaign reviewed, the pages/equations/conventions consumed, and the difference between the published result and the SM's adaptation. The evidence behind every row is the cold source-audit record copied into `audit/` (one lane per registry entry, sealed 2026-09-06 on frame SM6; the registry entries and all six literature nodes are byte-identical in SM12): `ACCESS_AUDIT.md` (which file, which hash, which pages were read), `T1_STATEMENT.md` (the published statement at the cited locator against the SM's text), `T2_DELTA.md` (what was read at proof depth and every gap named), `CONSUMPTION_MAP.md` (SM locator ↔ source locator, clause by clause), `RECOMMENDATION.md` (the seat's verdict). Read those before formalizing an axiom; this page is the map, not the evidence.

Terminology of the audits: **T1** = the statement at the cited page says what the SM uses; **T2** = its published proof was also read and establishes it; **rule 8** = a campaign proof never substitutes for a published one.

`files/` holds the copies whose license permits sharing (arXiv author versions), named `<author>_<year>_arXiv_<id>.pdf`, with `SHA256SUMS`. Everything else is identified in `NOT_INCLUDED.md` with its full bibliographic record, the pages consumed, and the hash of the copy the campaign reviewed, so you can obtain the same edition. Open access questions inherited from the campaign are in `OPEN_ITEMS.md`.

## B. Published result versus the SM's adaptation, per interface

- **L-1, HOMFLY–PT.** LM prove existence *and* uniqueness of P(K) ∈ Z[l^±1, m^±1] on based ordered oriented projections with the skein lK₊ + l⁻¹K₋ + mK₀ = 0 and unknot value 1. The SM states `lit:homfly` in the variables a, z over Z[a^±1, z^±1] with aH₊ − a⁻¹H₋ = zH₀ on oriented diagrams and **without** uniqueness. Deltas, all disclosed by the SM: the substitution l = ia, m = −iz and the fact that the image lands in Z[a^±1, z^±1] are the SM's own one-page algebra (`lp:coefficient-transport`); LM's per-level invariance (Prop. 4(n)) is turned into the SM's level bookkeeping; uniqueness is imported separately (`lp:lm-uniqueness`) from LM p. 120 together with Reidemeister's theorem. ## C. The finite front-word reduction (`ng:finite-word`), stated precisely

**Published.** Ng (2007) proves a HOMFLY–PT a-degree bound for Legendrian/transverse links by an induction over fronts: Theorem 2 (unoriented) and Theorem 1 (oriented, "a minor modification"), driven by **Lemma 1**, which Ng attributes to Rutherford (Lemma 3.3 of the IMRN version): *via skein crossing changes, Legendrian isotopy, Legendrian destabilization and removal of trivial unknots, a front F can be turned into one with lower s, or the same s and lower s′* (s = number of singularities, s′ = singularities right of the rightmost left cusp). Ng **sketches** the proof: two sentences of setup, Figure 1 (p. 7), and the sentence "in each case … yields a simpler front" (p. 8). The case analysis lives in the figure and in Rutherford's elementary-word proof (arXiv v1 Lemma 3.2, pp. 8–12; the published IMRN Lemma 3.3 is not on file, E-25-5). Ng's Corollary 1 (the HOMFLY–PT instance of Theorem 1) is one sentence; **the SM does not consume it**.

**The SM's adaptation.** `ng:finite-word` is not a restatement of Ng's lemma. It asserts, for an *actual finite front word* on the SM's own front-word domain (`ng:front-domain`), that Ng's procedure and Rutherford's elementary-word proof supply a **finite principal chain** built from three named move types — typed disjoint-gadget commutations with the three local front moves (`ng:front-I/II/III`), the cusp-skein crossing interchange (`ng:cusp-words`) or its reflection, and zigzag deletion / the crossed-cusp shortcut (`ng:deletions`) / deletion of a separated standard front circle — with the stop rule "stop at the first strict decrease of s or at the standard-circle base; before that the selected procedure does not increase s." The rest of the `ng:` chain (nine statements) is the campaign's own transcription and is **proved in the SM**; in particular the degree induction `ng:local-front-bound` (sm-3:2304) runs over the SM's quantity B(F) = D(F) − w(F) − d(F) − 1, proved internally, not over Ng's c↓ − ĩ. The SM states at sm-3:2297–2300 that the local certificates and the degree induction are separate arguments. The correspondence with Ng is structural, not verbatim.

**What this means in Lean.** The axiom is exactly the finite-chain statement of `ng:finite-word` on the SM's domain, no more: it must not be strengthened to Ng's Theorem 1/2 nor to Rutherford's lemma on abstract fronts. Everything downstream of it is a theorem to prove. Where the reduction's "in each case" is carried by a figure in the sources, the formalizer should expect to enumerate the cases; a gap there is a `proof-gap` report against `ng:finite-word`'s consumers, not a change to the axiom. Consumer route and every locator: `audit/L-4_EXIT/CONSUMPTION_MAP.md` §0; proof-depth reading: `audit/L-4_EXIT/T2_DELTA.md` §1.

## D. Modelling note for the formalization

The SM's literature inputs are stated on **diagrams** (Reidemeister invariance and descent). If links are defined in Lean as diagrams modulo the three Reidemeister moves and planar isotopy, then Reidemeister's theorem (the identification of that quotient with ambient isotopy classes) is outside the formal scope and need not be admitted; only the diagram-level statements of `lit:homfly`, `lp:lm`, `lp:lm-uniqueness` are axioms. If links are defined topologically, Reidemeister's theorem must be added as an axiom, and its printed proof is not on file in this campaign (E-25-6; Queffelec 2024, `files/`, is a modern proof the campaign holds but did not read at depth).

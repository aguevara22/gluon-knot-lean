## 1. The gap, precisely

* Where it bites. cp:finite-contact-path's proof (sm-3:3234-3326) builds the flattened family H_s, extends its
  velocity to a compactly supported ambient field, flows it, and says: "Thus its endpoint gives an actual ambient
  isotopy between the two oriented links. The retained global source premise yields H_{D_ε} = H_{D_T}. … This
  step explicitly consumes the global L1 premise, Literature input lit:homfly." The premise consumed is lit:homfly's
  fifth clause "Its value depends only on the oriented link presented by D".
* What is frozen. The accepted `SM.lit_homfly` renders that clause as `HomflyClauses.descent` over `LinkEquiv`, the
  equivalence generated by planar isotopy and the three moves (D2, SOURCES/INDEX.md §D). The step needs: two polygonal
  diagrams whose spatial readings are ambient-isotopic are `LinkEquiv`. That is Reidemeister's theorem (for smooth
  isotopies of the polygonal readings), excluded by D2; no sixth axiom (policy). Hence GAP-2 is a consequence of a
  documented design decision on an existing interface, not an oversight of the rows.
* GAP-1 (FINAL §8 risk 5: `P` is evaluated on smooth projections, only polygonal `Diagram`s exist) is CLOSED by
  design, not by policy: FR-1 (accepted for ng:front-domain, cf:lem-rounding, def:transverse-front) reads every smooth
  diagram through a record-carrying polygonal `Diagram`, and `P` depends on the record alone (rp:record-polynomial,
  accepted). The sketch supplies the two missing carriers: `SmoothKnotDiagram.Carries` (transverse fronts, over =
  the diagram's `isOver`) and `SpatialLink.HeightMarking` (multi-component projections, over = smaller `y`).
* Statement of the missing clause (sketch §6, a `def … : Prop`, NOT an axiom, so the report can name it):
  `SM.AmbientIsotopyDescent := ∀ F : SpatialFamily c, (F.G 0).RegularGenericProjection → (F.G 1).RegularGenericProjection →
  ∀ X₀ X₁, Nonempty (HeightMarking (F.G 0) … X₀) → Nonempty (HeightMarking (F.G 1) … X₁) → LinkEquiv X₀ X₁`.
  With it as an explicit hypothesis, row 91 reduces to 89 + 90 + `lit_homfly` (a ~500-line proof); without it 91 is
  unprovable under the frozen interfaces.

### (a) CV:ax:etnyre — rendering: option (iii), the used consequence; (i) and (ii) rejected

Printed (d10_axioms.tex:387-397): "Let T be a front diagram of a transverse knot with no downward vertical tangency.

reads the identity only as a bridge from the diagram's writhe to the transverse HOMFLY–PT bound of Axiom ax:slbound.

  is presupposed, not made). Its *content* is nevertheless exactly what the CV text uses (see (iii)).
* (ii) opaque `sl : TransverseKnot → ℤ` + axiom — NOT ALLOWED: a sixth literature axiom; the CV ax:* rows are theorems

  the transverse HOMFLY–PT bound". The faithful CV-lane object is therefore the composite of 161 and 162 with `sl`

## What is rendered how (recorded for the reviewer)

* "a map D ↦ H_D(a,z) ∈ ℤ[a^{±1},z^{±1}] on oriented link diagrams" (lit:homfly) is a function
  `Diagram → R` on the polygonal oriented link diagrams of `SM.LinkDiagram` (def:positive-lift's
  first sentence), `R = ℤ[a^{±1},z^{±1}]` of `SM.LinkLaurentRing`; "in ℤ[l^{±1},m^{±1}]" (lp:lm)
  is the DISTINCT type `T` of the same module, so no statement over `R` can leak into
  lp:lm-uniqueness ("The ring is the source's: no statement over ℤ[a^{±1},z^{±1}] is imported
  here", AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:72-73).
* "planar isotopy" / "positive page isotopy" / "modulo plane isotopy" is `PlanarIsotopic`; "the
  three Reidemeister moves" / "each actual ordinary oriented Reidemeister move" are `RI`, `RII`,
  `RIII`; "skein triple" is `IsSkeinTriple` (D₋ literally the switch of D₊ at a positive crossing,
  D₀ an oriented smoothing there); "the oriented link presented by D" is the `LinkEquiv` class —
  all from `SM.LinkMoves`, whose header records the readings (SOURCES/INDEX.md §D: links are
  diagrams modulo the moves and planar isotopy; Reidemeister's theorem is outside the formal scope
  and no sixth axiom is admitted).
* "the crossing-free circle" (lit:homfly) and "the unknot" (lp:lm-uniqueness) are every
  one-component crossing-free diagram, `Diagram.IsCrossingFreeCircle` (design decision D10: no
  Jordan/Schoenflies theorem is needed to state the clause this way).
* "the function constructed in Section 1 of Lickorish--Millett, not an arbitrary function with
  similar values" (lp:lm): Lean cannot name LM's construction.  The rendering fixes ONE witness of
  `lp_lm` once and for all, `lmF := Classical.choose lp_lm`, and states lp:lm-uniqueness relative
  to that witness (`∀ Q, LMCompetitor Q → Q = lmF`).  Any function with lp:lm's properties is a
  competitor in the sense of lp:lm-uniqueness (theorem `LMClauses.competitor`), hence equals `lmF`
  (theorem `eq_lmF_of_lmClauses`); so the chosen witness is, up to the identification the
  registry itself makes on p. 120 of the source, the source function, and the two readings ("∀
  witnesses" / "the chosen witness") coincide (design fidelity risk 4).
* "independently of the auxiliary component order, nonsingular basepoints, and order of required
  crossing switches" (lp:lm): `F` takes only the diagram `D` — neither a `Basing` nor any order of
  crossing switches is a parameter of `F` — and the initialization is quantified over EVERY basing
  `B : D.Basing` (component order + nonsingular basepoints, `SM.LinkDiagram`).  The "order of
  required crossing switches" is the source's construction device; nothing in the statement
  depends on it, so it appears nowhere in `LMClauses`.
* "In particular every crossing-free c-component diagram has that value, regardless of nesting or
  orientations. Here c ≥ 1." (lp:lm) is a theorem (`LMClauses.crossingFree`): a crossing-free
  diagram is UNDER-first for every basing and every diagram has a basing; `c ≥ 1` is
  `Diagram.componentCount_pos`, so `c - 1` is a natural exponent.
* "For a supplied finite sequence, take the maximum of the finitely many crossing counts and apply
  source Proposition 4 at that level one move at a time. No bound by the endpoint crossing counts
  is required. The imported construction and its Propositions 1--6 stop before the final p120
  inference from arbitrary ambient isotopy to a finite move sequence." (lp:lm, AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:
  44-48) is the source's proof commentary: the per-move fields give invariance under any finite
  sequence by induction (`LMClauses.descent`), and nothing about ambient isotopy is stated.
* lp:lm-uniqueness's hypotheses on a competitor are the registry's own operative sentence, "A map
  on diagrams that is invariant under planar isotopy and the three Reidemeister moves depends only
  on the isotopy class of the oriented link by Reidemeister's theorem ... the uniqueness therefore
  applies to every such invariant map with the source skein and the unknot value"
  (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:67-72): `LMCompetitor Q` = planar + the three moves + unknot + source skein.
  "with no restriction on the support or on the coefficients of a competitor" = `Q` ranges over all
  of `Diagram → T`.
* Sanity: `lit_homfly_descent_redundant` proves the `descent` field of `HomflyClauses` from the four
  invariance fields (EqvGen induction on `LinkEquiv`), so `lit_homfly` is not stronger than its
  printed clauses (`homflyClauses_iff_printed`); `lmF_competitor : LMCompetitor lmF` shows the
  uniqueness axiom's hypotheses are met by its own distinguished witness.

TeX in the quotations below is rendered in Unicode (ℤ, ±, ⁻¹, ≥); the words are the registry's. -/

/-! ## lit:homfly — the HOMFLY--PT polynomial (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:10-17, sm-3:916-923) -/

/-- The printed clauses of lit:homfly (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:11-15, sm-3:917-921), one field per
clause, for a candidate map `H : Diagram → R` ("There is a map D ↦ H_D(a,z) ∈ ℤ[a^{±1},z^{±1}] on
oriented link diagrams that ...", AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:11-12). -/
structure HomflyClauses (H : Diagram → R) : Prop where
  /-- "invariant under the three Reidemeister moves" (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:12, sm-3:918), move I. -/
  reidemeister_I : ∀ D D' : Diagram, RI D D' → H D = H D'
  /-- "invariant under the three Reidemeister moves" (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:12, sm-3:918), move II. -/
  reidemeister_II : ∀ D D' : Diagram, RII D D' → H D = H D'
  /-- "invariant under the three Reidemeister moves" (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:12, sm-3:918), move III. -/
  reidemeister_III : ∀ D D' : Diagram, RIII D D' → H D = H D'
  /-- "invariant under ... planar isotopy" (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:12-13, sm-3:918-919). -/
  planar : ∀ D D' : Diagram, PlanarIsotopic D D' → H D = H D'
  /-- "takes the value 1 on the crossing-free circle" (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:13, sm-3:919): every
  one-component crossing-free diagram (`Diagram.IsCrossingFreeCircle`, design decision D10). -/
  circle : ∀ D : Diagram, D.IsCrossingFreeCircle → H D = 1
  /-- "satisfies aH_{D₊} − a⁻¹H_{D₋} = zH_{D₀} for every skein triple" (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:13-14,
  sm-3:919-920), on the relational `IsSkeinTriple`. -/
  skein : ∀ Dp Dm D0 : Diagram, IsSkeinTriple Dp Dm D0 →
    R.a * H Dp - R.aInv * H Dm = R.z * H D0
  /-- "Its value depends only on the oriented link presented by D" (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:14-15,
  sm-3:920-921): `LinkEquiv` is "the oriented link presented by D" (SOURCES/INDEX.md §D).  This
  field is implied by the four invariance fields (`lit_homfly_descent_redundant`). -/
  descent : ∀ D D' : Diagram, LinkEquiv D D' → H D = H D'

/-- **lit:homfly** (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:10-17, sm-3:916-923; policy name `SM.lit_homfly`):
"There is a map D ↦ H_D(a,z) ∈ ℤ[a^{±1},z^{±1}] on oriented link diagrams that is invariant under
the three Reidemeister moves and planar isotopy, takes the value 1 on the crossing-free circle, and
satisfies aH_{D₊} − a⁻¹H_{D₋} = zH_{D₀} for every skein triple. Its value depends only on the
oriented link presented by D."  (No uniqueness clause: "the former uniqueness clause over
ℤ[a^{±1},z^{±1}] was stated in no source ... and was struck from the statement in round 4",
AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:16.)  Existence in ∃-form; the clauses are the fields of `HomflyClauses`. -/
axiom lit_homfly : ∃ H : Diagram → R, HomflyClauses H

/-- The HOMFLY--PT polynomial `D ↦ H_D(a,z)` of lit:homfly: one witness of `lit_homfly`, fixed once
by `Classical.choose`. -/
noncomputable def homfly : Diagram → R := Classical.choose lit_homfly

/-- `homfly` satisfies every printed clause of lit:homfly. -/
theorem homfly_spec : HomflyClauses homfly := Classical.choose_spec lit_homfly

/-! ## lp:lm — the exact local LM construction (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:25-50, sm-3:935-960) -/

/-- The printed clauses of lp:lm (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:26-44, sm-3:936-954), one field per clause,
for a candidate map `F : Diagram → T` ("let F_D(l,m) be the function constructed in Section 1 of
Lickorish--Millett, not an arbitrary function with similar values. The source constructs it in
ℤ[l^{±1},m^{±1}], independently of the auxiliary component order, nonsingular basepoints, and order
of required crossing switches.", AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:26-30).  `F` takes only the diagram: the
component order and the basepoints enter only through the universally quantified `Basing` of
`underFirst_init`, and the order of required crossing switches is not a parameter at all. -/
structure LMClauses (F : Diagram → T) : Prop where
  /-- "It is defined modulo positive page isotopy" (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:31, sm-3:941). -/
  planar : ∀ D D' : Diagram, PlanarIsotopic D D' → F D = F D'
  /-- "satisfies l F_{D₊} + l⁻¹ F_{D₋} + m F_{D₀} = 0, μ = −(l+l⁻¹)m⁻¹" (lp:source-skein,
  AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:31-35, sm-3:941-945), on the relational `IsSkeinTriple`; `μ` is `T.mu`. -/
  sourceSkein : ∀ Dp Dm D0 : Diagram, IsSkeinTriple Dp Dm D0 →
    T.l * F Dp + T.lInv * F Dm + T.m * F D0 = 0
  /-- "and has the initialization F_D = μ^{c−1} for every based ordered c-component UNDER-first
  diagram. UNDER-first means that, traversing components in their order, from their basepoints and
  in their orientations, the first encounter with each crossing is the underpass. ... Here c ≥ 1."
  (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:36-41, sm-3:946-951): for every basing `B` (component order and nonsingular
  basepoints) under which `D` is UNDER-first, `F D = μ^{c−1}` with `c = D.componentCount ≥ 1`.
  Quantifying over every basing renders "independently of the auxiliary component order,
  nonsingular basepoints" (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:29-30). -/
  underFirst_init : ∀ (D : Diagram) (B : D.Basing), D.UnderFirst B →
    F D = T.mu ^ (D.componentCount - 1)
  /-- "The same function is invariant under each actual ordinary oriented Reidemeister move"
  (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:43-44, sm-3:953-954), move I. -/
  reidemeister_I : ∀ D D' : Diagram, RI D D' → F D = F D'
  /-- "The same function is invariant under each actual ordinary oriented Reidemeister move"
  (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:43-44, sm-3:953-954), move II. -/
  reidemeister_II : ∀ D D' : Diagram, RII D D' → F D = F D'
  /-- "The same function is invariant under each actual ordinary oriented Reidemeister move"
  (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:43-44, sm-3:953-954), move III. -/
  reidemeister_III : ∀ D D' : Diagram, RIII D D' → F D = F D'

/-- **lp:lm** (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:25-50, sm-3:935-960; policy name `SM.lp_lm`): "For every such
diagram D, let F_D(l,m) be the function constructed in Section 1 of Lickorish--Millett, not an
arbitrary function with similar values. The source constructs it in ℤ[l^{±1},m^{±1}],
independently of the auxiliary component order, nonsingular basepoints, and order of required
crossing switches. It is defined modulo positive page isotopy, satisfies
l F_{D₊} + l⁻¹ F_{D₋} + m F_{D₀} = 0, μ = −(l+l⁻¹)m⁻¹, and has the initialization F_D = μ^{c−1} for
every based ordered c-component UNDER-first diagram. ... The same function is invariant under each
actual ordinary oriented Reidemeister move."  Existence in ∃-form over the source ring `T`; the
clauses are the fields of `LMClauses`.  The source function itself is the witness fixed by `lmF`
(see the module header and `eq_lmF_of_lmClauses`). -/
axiom lp_lm : ∃ F : Diagram → T, LMClauses F

/-- The local LM function `D ↦ F_D(l,m)` of lp:lm: one witness of `lp_lm`, fixed once by
`Classical.choose`.  This is the rendering of "the function constructed in Section 1 of
Lickorish--Millett, not an arbitrary function with similar values" (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:26-28): Lean

theorem lmF_spec : LMClauses lmF := Classical.choose_spec lp_lm

  /-- "invariant under ... the three Reidemeister moves" (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:68, sm-3:972),

  /-- "invariant under ... the three Reidemeister moves" (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:68, sm-3:972),

/-- **lp:lm-uniqueness** (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:58-75, sm-3:962-979; policy name

/-! ## Sanity theorems: the axioms are not stronger than their printed clauses -/

/-- Sanity (design decision D4, fidelity risk 4): the `descent` field of `HomflyClauses` follows
from the four invariance fields by induction on the equivalence relation `LinkEquiv =
EqvGen (PlanarIsotopic ∨ RI ∨ RII ∨ RIII)`.  Hence `lit_homfly` asserts nothing beyond its printed
clauses "invariant under the three Reidemeister moves and planar isotopy" — the last sentence "Its
value depends only on the oriented link presented by D" is redundant in the formal reading of
"the oriented link presented by D" as the `LinkEquiv` class. -/
theorem lit_homfly_descent_redundant (H : Diagram → R)
    (planar : ∀ D D' : Diagram, PlanarIsotopic D D' → H D = H D')
    (reidemeister_I : ∀ D D' : Diagram, RI D D' → H D = H D')
    (reidemeister_II : ∀ D D' : Diagram, RII D D' → H D = H D')
    (reidemeister_III : ∀ D D' : Diagram, RIII D D' → H D = H D') :
    ∀ D D' : Diagram, LinkEquiv D D' → H D = H D' := by
  intro D D' h
  induction h with
  | rel x y hxy =>
    rcases hxy with hxy | hxy | hxy | hxy
    · exact planar x y hxy
    · exact reidemeister_I x y hxy
    · exact reidemeister_II x y hxy
    · exact reidemeister_III x y hxy
  | refl x => rfl
  | symm x y _ ih => exact ih.symm
  | trans x y z _ _ ih₁ ih₂ => exact ih₁.trans ih₂

/-- The six printed clauses of lit:homfly (without the descent sentence) already give
`HomflyClauses`. -/
theorem HomflyClauses.of_printed (H : Diagram → R)
    (planar : ∀ D D' : Diagram, PlanarIsotopic D D' → H D = H D')
    (reidemeister_I : ∀ D D' : Diagram, RI D D' → H D = H D')
    (reidemeister_II : ∀ D D' : Diagram, RII D D' → H D = H D')
    (reidemeister_III : ∀ D D' : Diagram, RIII D D' → H D = H D')
    (circle : ∀ D : Diagram, D.IsCrossingFreeCircle → H D = 1)
    (skein : ∀ Dp Dm D0 : Diagram, IsSkeinTriple Dp Dm D0 →
      R.a * H Dp - R.aInv * H Dm = R.z * H D0) :
    HomflyClauses H where
  reidemeister_I := reidemeister_I
  reidemeister_II := reidemeister_II
  reidemeister_III := reidemeister_III
  planar := planar
  circle := circle
  skein := skein
  descent := lit_homfly_descent_redundant H planar reidemeister_I reidemeister_II reidemeister_III

/-- `HomflyClauses H` is equivalent to the conjunction of the six printed clauses without the
descent sentence: the axiom `lit_homfly` is exactly as strong as "invariant under the three
Reidemeister moves and planar isotopy, takes the value 1 on the crossing-free circle, and satisfies
the skein relation for every skein triple". -/
theorem homflyClauses_iff_printed (H : Diagram → R) :
    HomflyClauses H ↔
      (∀ D D' : Diagram, PlanarIsotopic D D' → H D = H D') ∧
      (∀ D D' : Diagram, RI D D' → H D = H D') ∧
      (∀ D D' : Diagram, RII D D' → H D = H D') ∧
      (∀ D D' : Diagram, RIII D D' → H D = H D') ∧
      (∀ D : Diagram, D.IsCrossingFreeCircle → H D = 1) ∧
      (∀ Dp Dm D0 : Diagram, IsSkeinTriple Dp Dm D0 →
        R.a * H Dp - R.aInv * H Dm = R.z * H D0) := by
  constructor
  · intro h
    exact ⟨h.planar, h.reidemeister_I, h.reidemeister_II, h.reidemeister_III, h.circle, h.skein⟩
  · rintro ⟨hP, h1, h2, h3, hc, hs⟩
    exact HomflyClauses.of_printed H hP h1 h2 h3 hc hs

/-- lp:lm's "invariant under each actual ordinary oriented Reidemeister move" together with
"defined modulo positive page isotopy" gives invariance along every finite sequence of such moves
and isotopies (the registry's "For a supplied finite sequence ... one move at a time",
AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:44-46, as a theorem): any `F` with `LMClauses` depends only on the `LinkEquiv`
class. -/
theorem LMClauses.descent {F : Diagram → T} (hF : LMClauses F) :
    ∀ D D' : Diagram, LinkEquiv D D' → F D = F D' :=
  fun _ _ h =>
    eq_of_linkEquiv F hF.planar hF.reidemeister_I hF.reidemeister_II hF.reidemeister_III h

/-- lp:lm (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:39-41, sm-3:949-951): "In particular every crossing-free c-component
diagram has that value, regardless of nesting or orientations. Here c ≥ 1." — a theorem: a
crossing-free diagram is UNDER-first for every basing (`Diagram.underFirst_of_isEmpty`) and every
diagram has a basing (`Diagram.exists_basing`). -/
theorem LMClauses.crossingFree {F : Diagram → T} (hF : LMClauses F) (D : Diagram)
    (h : IsEmpty D.Γ.Crossing) : F D = T.mu ^ (D.componentCount - 1) := by
  obtain ⟨B⟩ := D.exists_basing
  exact hF.underFirst_init D B (D.underFirst_of_isEmpty h B)

/-- The unknot value of any `F` with `LMClauses`: on a one-component crossing-free diagram the
initialization gives `μ^{1−1} = 1` ("takes the value 1 on the unknot", AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:63). -/
theorem LMClauses.unknot {F : Diagram → T} (hF : LMClauses F) (D : Diagram)
    (h : D.IsCrossingFreeCircle) : F D = 1 := by
  rw [hF.crossingFree D h.2]
  have hc : D.componentCount = 1 := h.1
  rw [hc]
  simp

/-- Every function with lp:lm's clauses is a competitor in the sense of lp:lm-uniqueness: the
planar and Reidemeister invariances and the source skein are fields of both, and the unknot value
follows from the initialization (`LMClauses.unknot`). -/
theorem LMClauses.competitor {F : Diagram → T} (hF : LMClauses F) : LMCompetitor F where
  planar := hF.planar
  reidemeister_I := hF.reidemeister_I
  reidemeister_II := hF.reidemeister_II
  reidemeister_III := hF.reidemeister_III
  unknot := hF.unknot
  sourceSkein := hF.sourceSkein

/-- Sanity: the distinguished witness `lmF` satisfies the competitor hypotheses of
`lp_lm_uniqueness` (so the uniqueness axiom is consistent with `lp_lm` on its own witness). -/
theorem lmF_competitor : LMCompetitor lmF := lmF_spec.competitor

/-- The two readings of "the function constructed in Section 1 of Lickorish--Millett" coincide:
ANY function with lp:lm's clauses equals the chosen witness `lmF` (design fidelity risk 4). -/
theorem eq_lmF_of_lmClauses {F : Diagram → T} (hF : LMClauses F) : F = lmF :=
  lp_lm_uniqueness F hF.competitor

/-- lp:lm-uniqueness pointwise: every competitor agrees with `lmF` on every diagram. -/
theorem lmF_unique {Q : Diagram → T} (hQ : LMCompetitor Q) (D : Diagram) : Q D = lmF D :=
  congrFun (lp_lm_uniqueness Q hQ) D

/-- A competitor "depends only on the isotopy class of the oriented link" in the formal reading
(`LinkEquiv`): the registry's Reidemeister-theorem sentence (AXIOM_REGISTRY.md:67-70) as the
proved statement it is once the class is the `LinkEquiv` class. -/
theorem LMCompetitor.descent {Q : Diagram → T} (hQ : LMCompetitor Q) :
    ∀ D D' : Diagram, LinkEquiv D D' → Q D = Q D' :=
  fun _ _ h =>
    eq_of_linkEquiv Q hQ.planar hQ.reidemeister_I hQ.reidemeister_II hQ.reidemeister_III h

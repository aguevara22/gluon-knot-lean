Outer compositions encode every possible selected interior set; the selected product is over physical interior positions, and every actual outer part carries the sum over **all** its raw inner compositions. No selected subset, gap, or one-leaf case is dropped, and there is no extra multiplicity.

The algebraic decomposition itself does not assert that an arbitrary X is independent of perturbation variables.

This is an unrestricted ring identity for arbitrary H,U,X and every interval, derived from the canonical actual-transform factorization through the disclosed positional helper. It is sufficient as an exact polynomial decomposition before the zero-gap argument; it does not assume or prove formal coefficient cancellation, inverse constancy or realizability of assignments.

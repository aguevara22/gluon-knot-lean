# Autonomous execution

The recipient owns execution. Read AGENTS.md, TARGETS.md and OPEN_WORK.md,
bootstrap once, then resume from work/STATUS.md and work/TASKS.json.

Choose implementation, dependencies, proof strategies and review assignments
locally. Use the supplied exact Lean/Mathlib pins. Retry official installs/cache
or build the pinned source; record any independently reviewed necessary pin
change. No historical campaign repository, named staff member or approval gate
is required. Use provisioned resources; do not invent credentials or buy services.

The DAG records explicit references plus documented additions, not a complete
semantic analysis. Read source context. Prove missing lemmas, reconstruct missing
R assembly, and translate CV interfaces into the five permitted SM literature
interfaces. Read-only public literature may be consulted without asking.

Preserve the source meanings: connected-component chambers; continuous wall
germs; all printed genericity, simplicity, transversality and child conditions.
Alternative representations need proved equivalence. Define both R propositions
without assuming their truth; every intermediate conditional result must expose
its R parameter. The final theorem has no such parameter. No new axioms,
opaque placeholders, vacuous domains or repaired statements may replace the goal.

On uncertainty, compare definitions and uses and seek independent review locally.
Record the decision and its evidence in work/AUTHOR_NOTES.md; never wait for an
answer. If fidelity cannot yet be resolved, retain separate candidates and work
on independent branches. For a suspected false statement, seek and independently
verify an exact counterexample. Put proposed repairs in work/repairs/ and report
the original as obstructed if verified; never mark it complete.

After every work unit, update STATUS.md, TASKS.json, lean-declarations.json and
evidence, and run tools/progress.py --once. Run the ten-minute reporter throughout
active execution as PROGRESS.md specifies. Resource/context limits require a
resumable checkpoint, not a new author question.

Before accepting a row, get independent source/type review with identities,
statement/definition hash and a substantive fidelity reason. Another agent or
session without authorship qualifies; disclose it. If review is temporarily
unavailable, queue it and keep proving. Type/body changes invalidate review.
The checker rejects unregistered axioms and sorryAx. Kernel decide is allowed;
native_decide and external certificates cannot enter the theorem library.

Deliver sources, pins, declaration map, reviews, current checker receipts and
progress history under work/delivery/. Completion requires the focused stage-1
check and independent fidelity review; a build or 100% reported statuses alone
does not establish the result. Keep reports concise and state remaining gaps.

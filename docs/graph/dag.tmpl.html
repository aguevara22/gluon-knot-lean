How this graph was built

  • Edges are the frozen blueprint's explicit references plus its documented semantic additions (blueprint/DEPENDENCIES.json, 443 edges over 191 rows; the extra supporting lemma lem:weak-open has none). The blueprint itself warns that unlabelled dependencies may exist.
  • Status comes from work/lean/lean-declarations.json: accepted means kernel-checked, mapped, and independently AI-reviewed with the statement hash bound in the final checker receipt. Numbers are tools/claims.py document order; rows outside that list carry none.
  • Two accepted rows deviate from the blueprint: cb:embedded-rotation was proved without lem:gauss-two-discs (decision D-ER1), and thm:uniqueness is accepted although its listed prerequisite prop:anchor-values is pending. Kernel-checked proofs cannot cite a pending row, so both are blueprint edges the proof route did not use.
  • Pending reasons are quoted from the completed fidelity review (FINAL_REVIEW.md §3).
